# Slice S-005: When someone runs `bash scripts/slug.sh` with a title over 200 characters, they see an error and exit 1.

<!-- senior-lead-Opus5.5-agent, 2026-10-13. Slice brief from the kit's TEMPLATE-SLICE.md. -->

Feature: [brief](brief.md)
Slice: 5 of slug

## Outcome
A title over 200 characters (the brief's longest expected title) is
rejected instead of slugged.

## Rules

AC-30 Given a title over 200 characters,
      when someone runs the command with it,
      then nothing is printed on standard output, standard error is exactly
      `slug: the title is over 200 characters; shorten it`, and it exits 1.

AC-31 Given a title of exactly 200 characters,
      when someone runs the command with it,
      then it prints its slug as before and exits 0.

## Out of scope
Any other change.

## Touch points
`scripts/slug.sh`.
