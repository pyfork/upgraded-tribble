# Slice S-001: When someone runs `bash scripts/slug.sh <title>`, they see the title as a lowercase slug.

<!-- senior-lead-Opus5.5-agent, 2026-10-12. Slice brief from the kit's TEMPLATE-SLICE.md. -->

Feature: [brief](brief.md)
Slice: 1 of slug

## Outcome
When someone runs `bash scripts/slug.sh <title>`, they see the title as a
lowercase slug: ASCII letters and digits, one `-` between words, none at
either end.

## Rules

AC-1  Given the title `Hello World!`,
      when someone runs `bash scripts/slug.sh "Hello World!"`,
      then it prints exactly `hello-world` and exits 0.

AC-2  Given a title with accented letters, punctuation and spaces at both
      ends, `  Café, au lait!  `,
      when someone runs the command with it,
      then it prints exactly `caf-au-lait` and exits 0: each non-ASCII byte
      is a separator.

AC-3  Given a title of 34 characters, `A long title with many words in it`,
      when someone runs the command with it,
      then it prints the whole slug, `a-long-title-with-many-words-in-it`,
      and exits 0.

## Out of scope
Cutting long slugs (slices 2 and 3).

## Touch points
`scripts/slug.sh` (new).
